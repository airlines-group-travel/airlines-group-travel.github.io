Of course, yes! The group booking feature is available on Southwest Airlines. Due to this, multiple travelers can fly together on a single itinerary and at a reasonable cost. Moreover, these Southwest Group Tickets are provided with a discount that is based on the group size and many other factors. Other than this, in this group booking feature, flyers can enjoy several advantages that are different and unique from regular bookings. ## **How to Book Southwest Airlines Group Travel?**

Enchanting an expedition with several flyers can be easily done on Southwest Airlines. To do so, the airline offers some simple ways by which the flyers can access the flights in no time. These methods include its official website and the contact number. Moreover, to learn about both of these methods, one can read the following subtitles.

#### **Book Southwest Airlines Group Travel Through the Official Website**

A structured way to get Southwest Airlines tickets for a group is its online platform. Through this, flyers can easily get Southwest Group Travel tickets quickly for any number of flyers, including more than 10. This is because the airline provides a step-by-step way to request a group quote from the official website. Moreover, one can also learn about its features, deals and current offers from here. Thus, the complete process for the same is mentioned herein.

- Locate the official web portal of Southwest Airlines.
- Go to the end and select the “Group Travel” icon.
- Now, click on “Request Proposal” and enter the required details in the form.
- Once submitted, the airline will share the price quote.
- Accept it before the given deadline and move on to make the payment.
- Share the travel details of all the accompanying travelers.
- And finally, get the confirmed itinerary by email.

#### **Reserve a Southwest Group Flight Via the Phone Call**

Getting the group tickets by communicating on a call is a very convenient way of booking. Through this method, the flyers can also share their requirements with the airline and certain booking-related doubts. Nevertheless, these individuals can only utilize such a process when the maximum number of flyers in their group does not exceed 25. As such, one of the members of the group can call 1-800-433-5368 to make the reservation.
