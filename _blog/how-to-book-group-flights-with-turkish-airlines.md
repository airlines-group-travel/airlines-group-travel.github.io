## **Does Turkish Airlines Do Group Booking?**

Indeed! The group booking feature is available on Turkish Airlines. Through this, tickets for several individuals can be booked together without creating individual reservations. Furthermore, travelers also benefit from Turkish Airlines group discounts by flying together. But to avail all these features together, they have to make sure to meet the airline's minimum passenger requirement and other travel conditions. Usually, the discounted fares and group booking option can be availed by 10 or more travelers flying for a common travel purpose on the same flight. Therefore, one can do group booking with Turkish Airlines and can also fly without breaking the bank by qualifying for the discounted fares.

## **How to Book Group Flights with Turkish Airlines?**

A traveler can make a group reservation with Turkish Airlines with ease. Moreover, the airline provides several ways to do so for passengers. This way, flyers can choose any suitable option that suits them best. Therefore, to make a Turkish Airlines group booking, the flyer can request a quote via its official email address or via a phone call. Both methods are effective and make it easy for each individual to follow along. Thus, a detailed explanation of each method is provided herein.



#### **Draft an Email to Book Turkish Airlines Group Ticket via Email**

To make the group flight reservation, a flyer can use the official email of Turkish Airlines. With this, they can share all the travel details and requirements in a clear format. By doing so, the airline will soon revert with a group quote, which one needs to accept before the mentioned deadline. At the end, one has to make the payment, and they will get the tickets o to their email.



#### **Make the Turkish Airlines Group Reservation Through Making a Phone Call**

Communication is an ideal option to book a group flight on Turkish Airlines. Through this, a flyer can directly share their travel details with the airline professionals by dialing +1 857 277 0361. Also, it becomes easy for them to understand the complete quotation of their booking. As a result, they can even ask any questions and get an instant solution.
