### **Does Emirates Do Group Bookings?**

Undoubtedly, yes! Emirates does provide a group booking program for travelers. With this feature, flyers can travel together without any stress or hassle of managing the trip. To make an [Emirates group ticket booking](https://www.airlinesgrouptravel.com/flights/emirates-group-travel-flight-booking)**,** the only thing required is that the flyers be in a group of at least ten members and share the travel details with the airline. With this, they not only get access to purchase the tickets but also to its various premium amenities and deals that are not provided to regular bookings. Thus, these flights make travel much smoother and more affordable because of the exceptional offerings.

## **How to Book Group Flights on Emirates?**

Emirates provides a straightforward and effective way to reserve group flights without going through a long procedure. To do so, the group leader has to navigate to the Emirates Group Travel online platform and share the travel information with them. Moreover, they also don't need to pay any additional fee or go through a long procedure. Therefore, the details and explanation of the complete booking procedure are defined in the following steps.

1. Head to the Emirates official web portal.
2. Tap the "HELP" icon and select the "Help and Contact" option.
3. Scroll down and click on the “Group bookings” option. 
4. Now, fill the form with all the necessary details and submit it. 
5. Before long, the airline will share a quotation to the registered email ID. 
6. Review it and confirm within the given time period. 
7. Thereafter, make the final payment and share the accompanying members' details with the airline. 
8. Finally, the airline will send the ticket itinerary to the email address.

### **Does Emirates Offer Group Discounts?**

Indeed, group discounts are offered on Emirates only to groups of travelers. These discounts reduce the fare price of the group flights, which is much lower than purchasing separate tickets. However, the Emirates group booking discount may vary from one group to another, as these are provided on the basis of the number of companions, travel destination, class, and much more. Along with this, they also have to comply with the conditions of the airlines.
