Airlines give travelers opportunities to make incredible memories by allowing them to fly with their companions. One such carrier is KLM, which is known to offer comprehensive travel services. Its group booking feature is especially designed for those willing to fly with their known members to common destinations. Furthermore, the KLM Group Booking option is available on all routes operated by the airline. So, travelers can conveniently fly together to different destinations and experience seamless travel. Additionally, they can check the information highlighted below to orchestrate shared trips with more ease.

### **Are There Group Discounts on KLM?**

Undoubtedly! Group discounts are available on KLM for those who ensure that their group has at least 10 passengers and that they all travel for the same purpose. When the booking request is sent by meeting the group size along with other guidelines of the airline, then these special fares can be accessed smoothly. Besides, one may find group discounts available on select international and domestic flights. Not only that, but depending on the travel season, class, and availability, these fares can fluctuate.

## **How Do I Book Group Travel With KLM?**

KLM allows groups of individuals to take its flights to reach their preferred location. Furthermore, it offers them a very convenient method to book seats for each member on the same flight simultaneously. Organisers can submit requests to purchase **[KLM Group Tickets](https://www.airlinesgrouptravel.com/flights/klm-group-booking)** online via its official web portal. However, this booking process differs from an individual one; passengers must follow it properly. Here are the detailed steps related to booking group travel with KLM.

1. Begin at the KLM Royal Dutch Airlines official website.
2. On the homepage menu, choose the "Information" section.
3. Opt for "Ticket Options and Services" and then "Travelling in a Group".
4. Further, submit the requested information in the application form.
5. KLM will then prepare a quotation; passengers have 14 days to respond to it.
6. They have to pay the deposit and then complete the payment for the flight tickets.
7. After that, the airline may need the full names of every group member.
8. Provide them and get a ticket sent to your registered email and phone number.

### **What Are the Perks of Booking KLM Group Flights?**

Travelers can fly with their companions on a KLM flight for different purposes. It can be for leisure, sports, business, school trips, and other occasions. Moreover, when multiple companions fly together on the same flight, they avail themselves of unparalleled perks. These KLM Group Travel benefits are very exclusive and are available when more than 9 flyers travel together. Besides, the advantages offered by booking a group flight are as follows.

- Everyone in the group gets the same fare.
- Get up to 14 days to confirm your booking.
- Benefit from flexible payment and cancellation options.
- Modify the names of passengers for free before tickets are issued.
- Secure standard seats at no additional cost.
- Add more flyers to your group later.
