### **What Are Group Booking Policies on Lufthansa?**

Traveling together with close ones provides much fun and an exhilarating experience with Lufthansa Airlines. However, to acquire its group services, the passengers have to align with the terms and conditions of the airline. These rules are specifically set for the group of voyagers, and complying with all of these provides several amenities for the journey. Furthermore, the details of all its guidelines are provided in the following sections.

- A group must consist of 10 or more passengers flying together. 
- Children under the age of 12 can only travel with accompanying adults. 
- Online check-in facility is available for group travelers. 
- Names of all flyers must be submitted 14 days before departure.



## **How Can I Book a Group Flight with Lufthansa?**

Lufthansa Airlines offers a quick and convenient method to reserve tickets for a large number of passengers. That is its official website, where passengers can make a Lufthansa Group Booking request by filling out a form. In this way, one can also get to know about the several offerings and deals of the airline. Aside from this, the booking steps for the same are provided in the pointers below.

1. Begin by searching the official website of Lufthansa.
2. Go to the "Menu" icon and select the "Book and Prepare" option.
3. Further, choose the "Group travel & Charter" option.
4. Now click on the "To group travel" link.
5. Fill in the form with all the necessary requirements.
6. In a while, the airline will provide a quote to your email.
7. Review and approve it before the given time limit.
8. Thereafter, complete the payment and share the flyers’ details.
9. And finally, the tickets will be shared to the email address.

### **Does Lufthansa Offer Group Rates?**

Indeed, group rates are provided on Lufthansa flights for parties traveling together. With these rates, passengers can fly to their desired location without breaking the bank. This is because the Lufthansa group discount is provided for the complete itinerary of group flyers, which is much better than purchasing the tickets separately. However, these discounts are provided under certain conditions so that they may vary for different groups.
