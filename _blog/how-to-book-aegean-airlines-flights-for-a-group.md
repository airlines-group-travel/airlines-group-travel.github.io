### **What Are the Group Booking Terms & Conditions on Aegean Airlines?**

Maintaining the flight for several individuals becomes easier when one follows some rigorous guidelines of Aegean Airlines. By fulfilling the [Aegean Airlines group travel policy](https://www.airlinesgrouptravel.com/flights/aegean-airlines-group-travel), all the companions can travel together smoothly on the air carrier without any complications. Further, these policies are strictly enforced for group travelers only, and non-compliance with these may result in cancellation of the itinerary. Thus, to understand these guidelines, read the pointers described at the bottom.

- A group must consist of at least ten passengers flying to the same destination.
- One can request extra seats with a new quote and airfare based on flight availability. 
- Any modifications to flight schedules can be made before departure and may require an additional fee. 
- Duplicate reservations are not permitted. 
- The group name must be submitted in Latin characters.

1. Search for a travel agency that deals in group bookings. 
2. Navigate to their official website and make a booking request.

4. After a while, a quotation will be sent to the email; approve it within the given time period. 
5. Later, make the payment and provide the names and other details of the companions. 
6. Ultimately, the ticket itinerary will be sent to the registered email ID.
