### **What Is the Group Booking Policy on EgyptAir?**

A traveler flying on an EgyptAir flight with their companions reaches their destination in full comfort. Furthermore, they also avoid any complications securing the seats for everyone flying together. This becomes possible because of the [EgyptAir group travel](https://www.airlinesgrouptravel.com/flights/egyptair-group-booking) terms and conditions. Through these rules, travelers gain insight into the requirements of booking a group flight and flying in larger numbers. Moreover, they can dive into those group travel rules from the points cited below. 

- More than 9 flyers are required to fly together on the same flight to qualify as a group.
- Changes in travel plans are allowed depending on seat availability and updated fares.
- Passenger details must be provided within the airline’s given time limit.
- Name corrections after ticket preparation may involve extra charges.
- Reservations may be withdrawn if the number of flyers drops below the required limit.
- Flyers who are flying in a group have to keep their necessary documents ready before takeoff.

## **How Can I Reserve a Group Flight with EgyptAir?**

EgyptAir makes it effortless for travelers to reserve a group flight. But the airline allows group travelers to take its group flight for 10 or more pax who are traveling for common events. Furthermore, they can raise an EgyptAir group request by placing a call at 202-301-8066. It is the official group booking contact number of the airline and lets travelers complete their reservation under proper guidance. Apart from this, Washington_groups@egyptair.com, this email address of the airline can also be used to submit the group booking request. Travelers choosing any of the methods have to follow some steps after sending their request, and those are as follows.

1. First, share the travel details and other required information either by phone or email.
2. Afterwards, wait for the price quotation, which can be sent to your email address.
3. Go through the offer and approve it before the deadline.
4. Thereafter, review the ticket price and pay accordingly. 
5. Further, provide the names of all group flyers to the airline via a suitable channel.
6. In the end, get confirmed flight tickets from the airline.

### **Does EgyptAir Offer Group Discounts?**

Absolutely! Group discounts by EgyptAir are offered for travelers willing to fly together with their companions. Furthermore, on both domestic and international flights, these fares are easy to grab. But to qualify for the EgyptAir group discount, 10 or more known flyers are required to fly together on the same flight segment. Apart from this, the airline may offer these special fares when the demand for travel is low to attract more flyers and fill their seats together.
