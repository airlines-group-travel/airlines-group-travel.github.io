### **What Is the Group Booking Policy on French Bee?**

A traveler can arrange shared trips with French Bee in a hassle-free way when they are aware of certain guidelines for group booking. Furthermore, the airline requires both international and domestic travelers to comply with these conditions for a better reservation and travel experience with companions. Besides, by calling a French Bee group number, one can become familiar with these rules. Furthermore, the points illustrated below also contain the group booking policy.

- Group reservations can be arranged on French Bee for 10 or more travelers heading out for the same purpose.
- Personal details of passengers may be updated if the airline's group fare conditions allow it.
- Traveling dates or flight schedules can be adjusted based on the terms agreed upon.
- The required amount for flight tickets has to be paid within the time limit provided by the airline. 
- Travelers need to keep their required papers and documents ready before departure.

## **How to Book Group Flights on French Bee?**

French Bee allows groups of travelers to take its flights for a number of occasions. On this airline, booking a single ticket for all members flying together is also easy. Organizers can do so by sharing the travel, group, flight, and other required details at the official email address of the airline. However, the email IDs for French Bee Group Booking vary by departure location. So, travelers have to opt for the one that suits their origin. Moreover, the addresses for a group reservation have been cited below.

- **Paris Orly, France:** groupes-paris@frenchbee.com
- **Reunion or Punta Cana:** groupes-reunion@frenchbee.com
- **New York, San Francisco, or Los Angeles (USA):** groupes-usa@frenchbee.com
- **French Polynesia:** groupes-ppt@frenchbee.com
- **Montreal, Canada:** groupes-ca@frenchbee.com
