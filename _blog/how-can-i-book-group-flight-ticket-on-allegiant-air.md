## **How Can I Book Group Flight Ticket on Allegiant Air?**

Allegiant lets flyers secure tickets for a group quite easily through the multiple modes offered. They could proceed with the booking by sending an email or visiting the official website of the airline. Either way could prove to be ideal for curating an [Allegiant Group Booking](https://www.airlinesgrouptravel.com/flights/allegiant-air-group-booking) depending on the preference of the travelers. To submit the request by drafting an email, use group.bookings@allegiantair.com and make the reservation without time pressure. Apart from this, the flyers can go to the official website and make their booking there in a few steps. Those steps are shown in the pointers below.

1. Open the official website of Allegiant. 
2. Scroll to the bottom and select the “Groups / Charters” option.
3. Click on the “REQUEST A QUOTE” icon. 
4. Now, choose the total number of passengers. 
5. Then, enter your email address and share the information about the journey. 
6. After that, the airline will share a tailored offer with a time limit. 
7. Confirm it before the due date and proceed with the payment. 
8. Share the names of the travelers and receive the tickets at your email address. 

## **Does Allegiant Offer Group Rates?**

Yes, Parties of 10 or more passengers traveling together with the same origin and destination can travel as a group on Allegiant Air. Flying with multiple members to a destination naturally makes the travelers eligible for a special reduction in the ticket prices of a shared trip. These Allegiant Air Group Rates make it possible to fly without breaking the bank, regardless of the number of flyers traveling. Still, these special airfares are subject to the attributes of each individual request and are calculated based on that.
