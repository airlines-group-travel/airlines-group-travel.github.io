Travelling to any place becomes more enjoyable and compelling with known companions. But securing seats for several passengers together under one PNR can be a challenging task. Not with JetBlue, as it offers a way to bring a bunch of flyers together through its group booking option. Furthermore, the JetBlue Group Booking option is suitable for all those passengers heading to the same place for any occasion. Moreover, this service of the airline possesses a number of exclusive and unbeatable features. However, certain requirements and rules are also attached to it. Travellers are required to have an understanding of those before organising an excursion. Besides, they can find detailed information about this option in the sections explained below.

### **Does JetBlue Offer Group Discounts?**

Absolutely! Group discounts on JetBlue are offered on almost all its operated routes. But the airline offers such deals when 10 or more adults have the same purpose to travel on the same flight. Besides, these rates fluctuate based on the season one has chosen to travel, route, availability, and various other factors. Therefore, travellers have to qualify and meet the airline's policies to save on their group bookings.

## **How Do I Book a Group Travel with JetBlue?**

On JetBlue, travelling with several passengers with a single ticket is effortless. Moreover, travellers can get such tickets by requesting a quotation. They can do so by submitting the **[JetBlue Group Travel Request Form](https://www.airlinesgrouptravel.com/flights/jetblue-group-travel-flight-booking)** available on its official website. Besides, one can use this method to book a flight anytime they want and from anywhere. The steps to proceed to book a group travel online are mentioned below.

1. Reach out to the official website of JetBlue.
2. Head over to the “Travel Info” on the home page.
3. Find the option “Group Bookings” and choose “Submit a request for a group booking”. 
4. Further, share the requested details in the form available on the next page.
5. The airline soon checks the request and forwards a quotation.
6. Group leaders have to accept it before the prescribed deadline.
7. After that, they have to submit a deposit and then make the full payment. 
8. Next, the airline may ask for group members' names, so add them accordingly.
9. Once done, the flight tickets will be delivered to your registered email address.

### **What Is JetBlue Phone Number for Group Travel?**

Travellers can also request group travel on JetBlue by using the call method. This allows them to get in touch with a live person and convey their booking requirements effortlessly. Not only that, organisers booking JetBlue Group Travel via this method will be able to learn about the deals and discounts available. Through this, they are able to manage their travel expenses easily. Furthermore, dialling the JetBlue contact number, which is 1-888-JETBLUE (1-888-538-2583), can also be a better option to get personal assistance for reservations. So, those looking for these facilities for making a group reservation can call the airline directly.
