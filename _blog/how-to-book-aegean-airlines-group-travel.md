## What Are the Group Travel Terms & Conditions on Aegean Airlines?

Aegean Airlines accommodates group journeys arranged for different travel purposes with specific guidelines. But the applicable conditions may vary as per the nature of the journey and the requirements attached to the booking. Besides, the [Aegean Airlines group travel](https://www.airlinesgrouptravel.com/flights/aegean-airlines-group-travel) policy covers essential aspects such as eligibility, payment conditions, passenger details, and other reservation formalities. A traveler going through them in advance can complete their booking and travel smoothly. The relevant provisions governing these group journeys are detailed in the sections below.

- The group expedition can be arranged for more than 9 flyers willing to fly on the same flight and toward the same route.
- Creating several group requests for the same traveler, route, and travel dates is not accepted. Also, such duplicate entries may be removed automatically.
- The group name should be entered correctly in Latin characters. Requests made with misleading, training, or speculative group details may be cancelled.
- A change in the flight date, timing, or itinerary is possible only when the individual group terms allow it. Any applicable fare difference or additional charge may then be collected.
- Passenger names need to reach the airline within the deadline mentioned in the group terms and should correspond exactly with the passport or identification document.

## How to Book Aegean Airlines Group Travel For a Group?

Aegean Airlines provides a route for arranging group reservations through a travel agency. The required group request can be shared with the agents by providing the relevant journey and passenger details for the intended itinerary. They can then coordinate these **Aegean Airlines group booking** details and proceed with the applicable reservation formalities. The complete procedure for making such reservations through a travel agency is outlined in the steps below.

1. Head to the website of a travel agency that offers group travel services.
2. Access the group travel form and enter the essential details.
3. Further, check the entered information and submit the request.
4. Then, your request will be reviewed, and a quotation will be sent to you.
5. Check the offer and confirm it within the given time.
6. Complete the payment according to the quoted amount and terms.
7. Afterward, submit the names of all members along with any other needed information.
8. After everything is received, the group itinerary is sent to the registered email address.

Yes, Aegean Airlines offers discounts for those passengers who fulfill the terms and conditions and travel on a shared journey.
