Embarking on a journey with close members fills one's mind with a lot of excitement. This excitement gets enhanced even more when the air partner is Cathay Pacific. In addition to flying with numerous travelers, the airline also offers a Cathay Pacific Group Booking feature that allows them to fly together. Through this group booking, the flyers do not have to make individual bookings, as the tickets are provided in a single itinerary. Also, this service offers various premium perks to the groups that align with certain conditions. Therefore, all the information on this is provided herein.

## What Are Group Booking Terms & Conditions on Cathay Pacific?

Traveling with a large group on Cathay Pacific requires everyone to align with some of its rules and regulations. These conditions allow the flyers to access the [Cathay Pacific Group Travel](https://www.airlinesgrouptravel.com/flights/cathay-pacific-group-travel-booking) benefits and also provide a smoother journey. Therefore, these conditions make it easier for everyone to get tickets at lower rates. Thus, one can learn them from the pointers below.

- A minimum of 10 passengers are required to fly in a group.
- Final payment and travelers' details have to be submitted before the prescribed deadline.
- Any changes to the flights depend on the conditions mentioned in the group quote.
- Discounts on flight tickets are based on group size, travel destinations, and other conditions.
- Not maintaining the airline's guidelines may lead to flight cancellation.
- The flyers traveling in a group are required to carry their travel documents during departure.

## How to Make a Group Booking on Cathay Pacific?

Cathay Pacific offers an ideal method to reserve group flights effortlessly in no time. One can do so by calling the airline group desk executives. In this way, the flyers can convey their travel details and requirements directly to the professionals and can also ask any questions. Further, the Cathay Pacific group department will assist the flyer with the quote, benefits and other features. Thus, the step-by-step procedure for the same is provided in the highlights below.

1. Contact Cathay Pacific by dialing 1 (833) 933-2244.
2. Select the appropriate language to communicate with the professionals.
3. Now, share the travel details and requirements with the airline.
4. In a while, the airline will share a group quote to the registered email address, and the professionals will explain it completely.
5. Approve the quote before the prescribed deadline.
6. Make the payment and share all the travelers' details.
7. Finally, get the confirmed ticket itinerary sent to the provided email.
