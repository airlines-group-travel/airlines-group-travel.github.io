### **Does Delta Offer Discounts for Group Travel?**

Yes, discounts are provided to travelers who are flying together with their group on Delta Airlines. But one must have at least ten members in the travel plan who share the same destination and flights to acquire this unmatched price cut. Due to these Delta group rates, it becomes highly possible to limit the travel budget according to the traveler’s requirements. Moreover, the discounted airfare is not fixed, as it changes on the basis of the distinct travel attributes of the group booking. 

## **How Do I Request Group Travel with Delta Air Lines?**

Securing seats for several travelers becomes extremely convenient when it is done on Delta Airlines. Here, the flyers will be able to pick any of the methods that are offered by the airline as per their preference. There is an option to book the flight through call mode by dialing 800-532-4777 from Monday through Friday, 8 A.M. to 8 P.M. It is perfect for those who want to connect with the Delta group sales desk in a rapid manner. Other than that, one can access the official platform of the airline and curate their booking as outlined in the pointers at the bottom.

1. Locate the official website of Delta Airline.
2. Tap on the “Need Help?” icon and then on “Special Circumstances”.
3. Now, select the “Get Assistance” option under the “Group Travel” tab.
4. Next, click on the “Book Group Travel Now” button. 
5. Then, fill out the information in each segment of the request form. 
6. Later, you will be presented with a quotation that has an expiry date. 
7. Confirm the price quote before the due date and make the payment. 
8. Further, share the names of the passengers along with other details. 
9. Finally, the tickets will be sent to your submitted email ID.
