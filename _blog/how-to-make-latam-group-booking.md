## **How to Make Latam Airlines Group Booking?**

A flyer wanting to create a reservation for several members in a smooth manner can do so effortlessly on LATAM Airlines. It gives the travelers an opportunity to go through the entire booking procedure on a dedicated portal. Handling the LATAM group booking this way allows one to acquire the flight tickets with zero issues. In addition, the entire ticketing procedure is outlined in the steps below, and by looking at them, one can create the reservation clearly. 

1. Head to the official website of LATAM Airlines: www.serieslatam.com.
2. Set up a new account or sign in with an existing one. 
3. Click on the “New” icon and enter the name of the group without gaps. 
4. Now, share the group size, origin, destination, and other information. 
5. Then, search for flights and choose one according to your preferences. 
6. Later, a priced quote will be made available within 24 hours of the request being submitted. 
7. Accept the offer before the deadline mentioned therein and pay the travel cost. 
8. Further, share the flyers’ names, and the tickets will be sent to your registered email ID.
