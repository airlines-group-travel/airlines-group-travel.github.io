Air travel with numerous companions on Finnair brings utmost enthusiasm and excitement to the experience. By selecting this, they can effortlessly integrate the schedule, fare, and flights of multiple members in a single reservation credential. ## Does Finnair Offer Group Booking?

Yes, Finnair does offer group bookings for parties of 10 or more people. By virtue of this, one can avoid the hassle that usually occurs when multiple members travel together and curate a flawless travel plan. It is not limited to that only; the flyers also obtain many complimentary perks that are not available elsewhere. This makes it possible to enjoy many advantages at cost-friendly airfares.

## How To Book Finnair Group Travel?

Finnair lets flyers procure their flight tickets for a group in a quite easy way. Here, they can leave all their travel arrangements for their shared trip to a travel agency that suits their requirements. To do so, one can either submit a request by calling or visiting the official website. Curating a reservation in this way could free the travelers from the stress of booking and also save time. Furthermore, the complete walkthrough of the entire booking process is depicted in the pointers at the bottom.

- Connect with the travel agency handling the booking.
- Share all the details of the expedition required.
- Then, receive the priced offer in your email with a due date.
- Accept the quotation within the given time frame.
- Pay the travel expense stated in the quote.
- Share the names of the passengers.
- At the end, the tickets will be sent to the email address.

## What Are the Benefits of Group Travel on Finnair?

On Finnair, multiple travelers can enjoy a host of benefits through every phase of the journey, from booking to boarding to the in-flight experience. These advantages are designed to make the air journey more enjoyable, and information about them can be found on the Finnair Group Travel Portal in a clear manner. Furthermore, the key perks of group travel with the airline are shared herein.

- The flyer gets a flexible payment schedule.
- Name changes are possible until the issuance of tickets.
- Each traveler gets one checked baggage and one carry-on baggage under a single ticket price.

Which cabin the group ends up in shapes most of these perks, so it is worth reading how [Finnair fare classes](https://www.airlinesgrouptravel.com/blog/finnair-fare-classes) work before accepting a quote. For longer sectors, [Finnair business class](https://www.airlinesgrouptravel.com/business-class/finnair-business-class) can be quoted for the whole party.
