### **What Is the Group Booking Policy on Qantas?**

Travelers can fly in a group on Qantas without a hitch by following some specific guidelines. This will help them understand the essential aspects of making a group reservation. Furthermore, travelers abiding by these [Qantas Group Booking terms and conditions](https://www.airlinesgrouptravel.com/flights/qantas-airways-group-booking) will also be able to avoid extra penalties caused by making changes or cancellations. So, those seeking to have a proper understanding of the group booking policy beforehand must consider the points shared below.

- Qantas allows 10 or more pax to travel together as a group to the same destination for a common reason.
- Around 20% of the total fare must be paid upfront to hold the seats.
- If fewer flyers travel later, the extra deposit can be adjusted in the remaining amount.
- The full payment does not need to be completed immediately, as a later payment option is available.
- Names and passenger details should follow official travel documents to prevent cancellation issues.

## **How to Book Qantas Airlines Group Flight?**

Qantas offers travel options that suit distinct passengers needs. Group travel is one of their offerings that allows several passengers to fly under one PNR without creating separate bookings. But one has to request a Qantas Group Booking Quote in order to secure seats in bulk on the same flight. Moreover, travelers can visit the official website of the airline and follow the steps cited below to raise a request and get final group flight tickets.

1. First, head over to Qantas's official website.
2. From the main screen, move to the "Book" option and proceed with "Flights."
3. Then, look for "Group travel" and tap "Request a Group Quote."
4. Further, add the necessary information, including passenger count and journey requirements, to the provided form.
5. Send your completed inquiry to the airline and allow some time for the fare proposal.
6. Now, secure the offer by paying the initial deposit.
7. Thereafter, arrange the outstanding payment before the due date mentioned by Qantas.
8. Next, pass on the travelers' names and get the tickets in the group leader's registered email inbox.
