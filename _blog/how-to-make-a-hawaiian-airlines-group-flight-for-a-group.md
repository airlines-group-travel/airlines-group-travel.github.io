## **What Are the Group Booking Terms & Conditions on Hawaiian Airlines?**

Air travel planned with Hawaiian Airlines can be initiated smoothly with any number of companions when everyone adheres to its certain guidelines. By aligning with its terms and conditions, it becomes easy for flyers to access [Hawaiian Airlines group Reservations](https://www.airlinesgrouptravel.com/flights/hawaiian-airlines-group-booking) and its various premium deals & discounts. These conditions not only give access to these perks but also make the passenger aware of the various protocols that help in making the journey smoother and hassle-free. Thus, one can understand all of these by reading the points mentioned at the bottom. 

- Qualifying as a group requires a minimum of 10 members to fly together. 
- Names and other mandatory info of all the flyers must be submitted before the provided due date. 
- Seat reservations start 11 months prior to the departure date. 
- Not aligning with any of its rules may lead to cancellation of the flight.

### **How To Book A Hawaiian Airlines Flight For A Group?**

On Hawaiian Airlines, flyers can seamlessly reserve a flight for a number of companions. To do so, one of the group members can contact the group desk team of the airline by dialing 1-800-367-8058 or head to its official website. By using any of these methods, they can make a Hawaiian Airlines group request and get a quotation as per their needs. Along with this, flyers can also get to know about the various deals and offerings of the airline. Thus, to understand its booking procedure through the online platfrom, simply follow the step-by-step instructions outlined below. 

1. Go to the Hawaiian Airlines official website. 
2. Click on the “Menu” icon on the homepage and then go to the “Book” option. 
3. Further, choose “Group travel” from the available options. 
4. Press the “Request Group Travel” button and share the travel details by filling out the application form. 
5. Thereafter, a group quotation will be sent to the registered email address. 
6. Review it and approve it before the prescribed time period. 
7. Next, share the accompanying members' details with the airline and finalize the booking with the payment. 
8. At last, the tickets will be shared to the registered email address.
