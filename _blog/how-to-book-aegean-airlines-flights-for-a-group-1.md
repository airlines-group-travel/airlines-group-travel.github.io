That is why one should get familiar with the group booking conditions that are illustrated below.

- Travelers are required to maintain the minimum passenger threshold of ten or more individuals to access the group service. 
- The carrier doesn’t offer any discount for children, and infants under the age of two will be charged 10% of the adult fare. 
- Group fares include 1 checked bag, a carry-on bag of 23 kg and 8 kg, respectively, and a personal item.
- Name changes and other modifications are allowed if they are mentioned in the contract shared by the airline.
